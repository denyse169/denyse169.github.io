01 / About

I learned operations on the front line.

In a crowded aircraft cabin, a weak process becomes a customer problem within minutes. That’s where I learned how closely operations and customer experience are linked.

As a Cabin Manager at RwandAir, I supervised 6–8 person teams serving 500+ passengers a week. I managed performance, compliance, service standards, and incident response. Earlier, as a pioneer intern on RwandAir’s new loyalty program, I built Excel tracking and documentation processes that helped enroll more than 1,000 members.

Today I put that experience together with analytical tools: SQL, Power BI, Tableau, and Excel for data; process mapping and root-cause analysis to diagnose problems; and Jira (Scrum) to deliver changes. I’m studying for a Master of Global Management at Thunderbird and building skills in prompt engineering, custom GPTs, and agentic AI so I can use AI to automate and improve the processes I analyze.

03 / Approach

How I improve a process.

  1. 1

    Understand

    Talk to stakeholders and customers to learn what “better” means to them.

  2. 2

    Measure

    Define KPIs and analyze the data to see how the process performs today.

  3. 3

    Map & diagnose

    Map the AS-IS process and find root causes, not just symptoms.

  4. 4

    Improve & automate

    Design the TO-BE process and look for places where AI and automation can help.

  5. 5

    Deliver & sustain

    Write requirements, test with UAT, and keep dashboards running so the gains last.
